## Join the Adaption Discord

For product questions, API help, troubleshooting, and guidance from the Adaption team and community, join our Discord:

**[Join the Adaption Discord](https://discord.gg/sHhG8kwVav)**

When asking for technical help, include a concise problem statement, relevant **dataset** and **run** IDs, timestamps, and any error messages or HTTP status codes.

<Callout variant="note">
  Do not post API keys, credentials, private dataset contents, or other
  sensitive information in Discord.
</Callout>

## Contact the team privately

For account-specific questions, billing, or support requests containing sensitive information, email the Adaption team:

**[support@adaptionlabs.ai](mailto:support@adaptionlabs.ai)**

Include your account or organization name and the relevant technical context so we can resolve the request faster.

<Callout variant="tip">
  For self-serve answers, use the [FAQ](/resources/faq) and **API Reference** in
  the sidebar.
</Callout>

## Platform updates and general inquiries

To **hear about releases, features, and announcements**—or to reach us for non-urgent, general topics—use the contact form on [adaptionlabs.ai](https://www.adaptionlabs.ai):

**[Contact us — Adaption](https://adaptionlabs.ai/contact-us)**

That page is the right place to subscribe or send messages that are not day-to-day technical support.
